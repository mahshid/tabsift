# Privacy Policy for TabSift

**Last updated: October 27, 2026**

TabSift is a browser extension that helps users find and close inactive browser tabs.

Your privacy is important. TabSift is designed to work locally in your browser without requiring an account.

## Information TabSift Accesses

TabSift accesses information about your open browser tabs, including:

- Tab titles and URLs
- Last accessed times
- Tab status, such as whether a tab is active, pinned, or playing audio

This information is used only to identify inactive tabs, display them in the extension, and close tabs you choose to remove.

## Data Storage

TabSift stores your preferred inactivity threshold locally using the browser's storage API.

Your tab information is processed locally and is not stored in an external database.

## Data Sharing

TabSift does not send your browsing information to external servers, sell your data, or share it with third parties.

TabSift does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

## Permissions

TabSift uses the following browser permissions:

- **Tabs:** To identify inactive tabs and close tabs selected by the user.
- **Storage:** To remember your inactivity threshold.
- **Alarms:** To periodically update the forgotten-tab count shown on the extension icon.

## User Control

TabSift never closes tabs automatically. You choose which tabs to close and confirm the action before they are removed.

You can uninstall TabSift at any time to stop the extension from accessing your browser tabs.

## Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated if TabSift's features or data practices change. Any updates will be published on this page.

## Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please open an issue in the TabSift GitHub repository.
